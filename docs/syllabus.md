**Calculations with Fractions** ("Show that…")

  smallest possible number of members of the badminton club. → 24 (LCM of 12 and 8)

**Fractions Questions**
- Starter (monkeys): "My four pet monkeys and I harvested a large pile of peanuts. Monkey A woke in
  the night and ate half of them; then Monkey B woke and ate one third of what remained; then Monkey C
  woke and ate one quarter of the rest; finally Monkey D ate one fifth of the much diminished remaining
  pile. What fraction of the original harvest was left in the morning?" → 1/2 × 2/3 × 3/4 × 4/5 = 1/5
- Task 1: ten calculations with fractions and mixed numbers (e.g. 9 − 2/3, 3 ÷ 2/5, 4 4/5 − 3 2/3,
  9 1/4 − 7 2/3, 7 2/3 + 3/5, 1/4 ÷ 3/10, 2 1/2 ÷ 1 1/3, 1/4 × 1 2/3).
- Task 2: fraction **magic squares** (rows, columns, diagonals have the same total) using values like
  4½, 3½, ¾, 2½, 2, 4, 1½, 1¼, ¼, 1.
- Task 3: "Here are three identical shapes. 4/7 of A is shaded. 3/5 of C is shaded. How much of B is
  shaded?" (A shaded from the bottom up to a line, C shaded from the top down to a lower line; B is the
  band between the two lines → 4/7 + 3/5 − 1 = 6/35.)
- "If a > b, then 1/a > 1/b. Is this statement true? Give your reason." (False for positive numbers:
  e.g. a = 2, b = 1 gives 1/2 < 1; dividing 1 by a bigger positive number gives a smaller answer.)

**Fractions with algebra / Further Fractions**: simplify e.g. 42xyz/56, 17cf/34c², 3xy/xz; multiply

## Methods & notation used in class (match these)
- Fractions: common denominators for + and −; mixed numbers → improper fractions first for × and ÷;
  "Keep, Change, Flip" (multiply by the reciprocal) for ÷; simplify fully; answers as mixed numbers
  when asked.
- Percentages: 10% / 1% building blocks; **multipliers** (increase by 20% → × 1.2; decrease by 30% →
  × 0.7); percentage change = change ÷ original × 100.
- Ratio: bar models (boxes) for sharing; "1 part = …"; part : part vs part : whole; combine a : b and
  b : c by making the shared part equal.
- Expressions: grid method for expanding; careful with negatives (−3(2k − 4) = −6k + 12);
  ≡ means "identical for all values".
- Substitution: brackets first, powers before × ÷, (−3)² = 9; e³ with e = −3 gives −27.
- Sequences: nth term of arithmetic sequence = (difference)n + (zeroth term). Quadratic: second
  difference ÷ 2 = coefficient of n²; subtract an² and find the linear remainder. Notation Uₙ.
